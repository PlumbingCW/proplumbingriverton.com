Emergency Hydro Jetting in Riverton, UT

A line that clogs again within weeks, or a cable that stalled partway, is the classic case for jetting. If sewage is coming up right now, call our 24/7 line, stop all water use, and keep people and pets out of that room until we arrive.

Hydro jetting in Riverton, UT is cleaning the inside of a drain or sewer pipe with high-pressure water from a nozzle on a hose, which scours grease, scale and roots off the pipe wall. Our licensed and insured plumbers camera the line first and quote a clear price before work starts.

Symptoms

Signs a cable alone won't solve the problem

Most people call about jetting after a snake has already failed. Sometimes a plumber ran a cable and it caught partway down the line. Sometimes the drain worked for a month and then slowed again.

Back again in weeks

A cable pokes a hole through the clog but leaves a coating on the pipe wall. New debris sticks to it.

Cable stalls at one spot

A stall may mean roots, a heavy grease mass, a joint that has shifted, or a break. A camera tells us which.

Several fixtures drain slowly

When the tub, toilet and washer all slow down together, the building drain or lateral is narrowing.

Gurgling from the main

Air pushed back through traps often shows up before water does. Treat it as a warning, not a quirk.

Roots seen on a past camera look

Roots return unless the opening they entered is dealt with, so jetting is maintenance, not a cure.

Neither tool is better in every case. For a one-time hair clog in a bathroom sink, jetting is overkill, so we'd point you to drain cleaning instead.

Safety first

When we will not jet a line

Water at jetting pressure is gentle on healthy plastic or cast iron and hard on pipe that is already failing. If the line is cracked, collapsed, or has a joint that has dropped, a jet can widen the damage or push debris and water into the soil around it.

That is why a camera pass comes first. If it shows a break, we stop and talk about sewer line repair instead of jetting through it.

  • Visible cracks, offset joints or a belly that holds water mean jetting waits until the pipe is assessed.
  • Pipe that is very old and brittle may need gentler cleaning or lining before any pressure goes in.
  • A line with no cleanout may need a short access fitting installed first.
How it works

What the jetter does to roots and grease

A jet nozzle has several rear-facing ports. The water pushes the hose forward and scrubs the wall as it pulls back, carrying loosened debris down toward the main.

Fine roots are shredded and flushed. Thick, woody roots may need a cutter head on a cable first, then a jetting pass to clean up. Either way, roots can regrow from the opening they entered, so a camera follow-up is part of the plan.

Urgent

What to do first when the main line is backing up

Stop every drop of water going down the drain. That means no flushing, no dishwasher, no laundry, and no long showers. Sewage rises at the lowest fixture in the house, usually a basement shower, tub or floor drain.

Keep children and pets away, wear waterproof boots if you must cross wet floors, and wash hands thoroughly afterward. If the backup is active, we can often arrange same-day service when available, even outside normal hours.

Local picture

Where jetting fits in a Riverton sewer setup

  • ✓Private lateral, not the main.Jordan Basin Improvement District (JBID) maintains the sewer main, and the line from your house to the main is yours. We jet the private side, usually from a cleanout.
  • ✓Older clay lines grow roots.JBID says clay sewer pipes from before the 1980s are prone to roots. If your house is that old, a jet-and-camera visit every so often can be cheaper than a surprise backup.
  • ✓Grease is a district concern too.JBID asks residents not to pour cooking grease down drains, even with a disposal. Jetting removes the buildup, but only changing the habit stops it.
  • ✓Beyond the cleanout.If standing water in your cleanout points to a blockage in the main, call JBID at (801) 571-1166 first. We handle the private side.

We also dispatch to Bluffdale and South Jordan, where the same district runs the mains.

Getting ready for a jetting visit
