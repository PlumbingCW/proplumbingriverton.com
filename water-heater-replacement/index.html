Right now

Tank failed? What to do right now

  • Gas smell: leave the building and call Enbridge Gas Utah at 1-800-767-1689 or 911 from outside before you call a plumber.
  • Close the cold water valve on the pipe entering the top of the heater.
  • Turn off the breaker for an electric heater, or the gas control knob for a gas one.
  • Attach a hose to the drain valve and run it outdoors only if the water has cooled and you are comfortable doing so.
  • Photograph the tank, the label and the damage for your insurance claim, then call the 24/7 line.

We schedule same-day replacement visits when available, so a failed tank does not have to mean days of cold showers.

The verdict

Signs the tank is past repair

Parts can be swapped, but the steel tank cannot be repaired once corrosion breaks through. Water leaving the shell is the clearest sign. The same applies when the repair history is getting long, because each part buys less time.

  • ✓Water seeping from the seam or base,not from a pipe fitting on top.
  • ✓Rust-colored hot waterthat keeps returning after a flush.
  • ✓A tank that rumbles or pops loudlyfrom heavy sediment, with hot water running out quickly.
  • ✓Repairs piling up,such as a second thermostat or element in a short stretch.
  • ✓A bulging or deformed tank,which means stop using it and call.

If you are on the fence, our water heater repair visit tells you honestly which side of the line your unit sits on.

The work

What a proper replacement includes

1

Shut down and drain

We close water, power and gas, drain the old tank and disconnect the supply, gas and vent connections.

2

Set the pan and new tank

A pan goes under the heater where a leak would damage finished space. Utah's amendment says a heater pan may receive discharge from the heater only, and its drain may tie into a soil or waste stack only when the code official permits, trapped and vented.

3

Strap it to resist earthquake motion

Utah Code 15A-3-305 calls for straps in the upper and lower third of the heater's height, anchored to resist sideways movement.

4

Connect and vent

New flexible connectors, a relief valve with a discharge line, and for gas, a checked gas connection and flue.

5

Test, then haul away

We fill the tank, check for leaks, set the temperature, test combustion on gas units and take the old heater with us.

Expansion tanks come up on many installs. A plumber confirms whether your system needs one, and we raise it before work starts.

Before you decide

Riverton factors that change a replacement

Riverton's 2022 water quality report lists average hardness of about 190 mg/L, roughly 11 grains per gallon. Scale builds faster in harder water, and it shortens the life of heating elements and tank bottoms. Pairing a new heater with a water softener is worth discussing if your tap tests hard.

Permits are the other local question. Riverton says a building permit is required for work on plumbing, gas and mechanical systems the code regulates, and you apply by emailing building@rivertonutah.gov.
