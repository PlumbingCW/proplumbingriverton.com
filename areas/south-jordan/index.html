Emergency plumbing from next door in South Jordan

Riverton Pro Plumbing is based at 2600 W 12600 S in Riverton, and South Jordan is the city that borders us to the north. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, and the route up Redwood Road is a short one.

South Jordan has two ZIP codes. Homes around City Hall on Towne Center Drive use 84095, and the postal service lists 84009 as the city's other ZIP. Either way, you call the same 24/7 line and describe the problem.

The city is big. The 2020 Census counted 77,487 residents, and Daybreak, the planned community, is part of the city. At that scale a pressure or secondary-water problem is often street-wide, so ask a neighbor before you assume it is only your house.

Backups

A backed-up drain: is it the fixture, your line, or the main?

The Jordan Basin Improvement District (JBID) maintains the public sewer main in South Jordan. The private line from your house to that main belongs to you. Where the blockage sits decides who fixes it, so find out before anyone digs.

JBID's own test is simple. Open the cleanout on your property. Standing water there points to a blockage between the house and the main, while a dry cleanout points to your house plumbing.

Older clay lines are prone to root intrusion, per JBID. If a camera shows roots or a cracked joint, sewer line repair may be the lasting answer, not a third cable job. Cooking grease is the other common cause, so wipe pans into the trash even if you own a disposal.

Yard side

Canal water, hissing valves and pressure that moves

South Jordan runs secondary water to a portion of the city, through about 96 miles of lines fed by Utah Lake water and four canals. The season runs roughly May 1 to October 15. The city says that water is unfiltered and that pressure varies with distance, elevation and how many neighbors are watering.

Maintenance is your job from the stop-and-waste valve onward, and you must stay in backflow compliance. A wet patch in the lawn or a hissing box in the park strip is often a secondary line, not your house supply. Closing the secondary valve is a quick way to tell the two apart.

Culinary water leaks hide differently. A meter that keeps turning with every tap off, a damp slab edge or a warm floor needs leak detection with listening gear, not a shovel. The city also audits backflow devices at properties with secondary access, so keep your annual test paperwork.

Hot water

When the tank fails: swaps, straps and the permit question

A tank that leaks from the shell cannot be patched, so the realistic fix is a replacement heater. A drip at a fitting or a failed valve is a different story and may be a repair. We confirm which one it is before quoting.

South Jordan's city FAQ says a permit is needed for a new water heater, and it does not address repairs. Utah's amendment to the plumbing code also requires heaters to be anchored or strapped against earthquake motion, with straps in the upper and lower thirds of the tank. Ask the city what your swap needs.

Hard water shortens heater life, but South Jordan's 2025 water report does not publish a hardness number. The district that supplies the city's water, Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District, states about 10 grains per gallon district-wide. Test your own tap.
