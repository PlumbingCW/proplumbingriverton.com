Spring startup

Spring: what the first canal water of the year exposes

Secondary water in Bluffdale comes from canal companies, not from the city. Bluffdale City's secondary water page names the Utah and Salt Lake Canal, Welby Jacobs, Utah Lake Distributing, East Jordan and South Jordan canals as sources. The companies set the startup date, so ask yours or the city instead of copying a neighbor's sprinklers.

Startup day is when winter damage shows itself. A hose bib that froze in January can look fine until the line is under pressure, then spray into the wall behind it. Open each outdoor valve slowly and look at the wall and the ceiling below it. A drip at the stem is a small repair, but water inside a wall is a leak detection job.

  • ✓Hose bibs.Open slowly, watch for spray behind the faucet, and check the inside wall.
  • ✓Main shutoff.Turn it fully closed and open again. A valve that sticks in April will fail you in a real emergency.
  • ✓Water heater and softener.Look for rust streaks or damp rings around fittings and the base.
  • ✓Sprinkler zones.A dead zone usually means a broken head, a cracked lateral or a clogged filter.
Summer on canal water

Summer: irrigation season and the backflow rules that come with it

Canal water is not drinking water, and it must never connect to the pipes you drink from. Bluffdale's Cross Connection Program inspects properties with secondary water access. Its 2024 water quality report says an RPZ backflow preventer is required where there is risk and must be tested every year.

If your irrigation touches the house supply in any way, ask the city which assembly it expects before anyone cuts a pipe. We install and repair plumbing-side connections, and we tell you plainly when a job needs the city's inspection first.

Spraying water in July is usually a sprinkler line, not the house supply. Find the valve where the irrigation line enters your yard and close it. If the spray keeps going, or the wet spot is on the house side, call us and we trace the line.

Summer is also when scale on fixtures gets noticed. Bluffdale's 2024 report lists average total hardness of 189.4 mg/L, close to the figure of about 190 mg/L in Riverton's 2022 report. If scale clogs your aerators and coats heater elements, a water softener is worth pricing, but test your tap first.

Fall shutdown

Fall: shutting the system down before the first hard freeze

1

Close the irrigation supply

Shut the secondary valve when your canal company ends the season, then open the drain points so the line empties.

2

Take the hoses off

A hose left on a bib keeps water pressed against the faucet, which is a common reason bibs split.

3

Blow out the sprinkler lines

Use a compressor with a regulator. Too much air pressure cracks fittings and heads.

4

Wrap exposed pipe

Check the garage, crawlspace and any pipe along an exterior wall. Insulate what you can reach.

5

Learn the main valve

Everyone in the house should know where it is and which way it turns.

Winter freeze

Winter: when a frozen pipe becomes an emergency

The nearest published weather normals are from Salt Lake City airport, not Bluffdale, so treat them as a guide. That station averages about 112 days a year with a low at or below 32 degrees F, and its January normal low is 24.2 F. Your street can run colder or warmer on any given night.

If a faucet runs dry in a freeze, open it and warm the pipe with a hair dryer. Never use an open flame. If a pipe has already split, close the main valve, leave it closed and call us. A cracked line inside a wall or under a slab needs water line repair by someone who can find the break, not a guess.

What we know about water, permits and sewer in Bluffdale

Drinking water

Bluffdale buys culinary water from Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District. The 2024 report lists hardness of 189.4 mg/L on average, so expect scale and test before sizing any softener.

Secondary water

Canal companies run it, not the city. Confirm start and stop dates with your company, because Riverton's March 15 valve shutoff is a different system.

Permits

Bluffdale's exempt list covers stopping leaks, clearing stoppages and reinstalling a toilet without changing valves or pipes. Replacing defective concealed piping needs a permit and inspection.

Sewer

Jordan Basin Improvement District serves Bluffdale, with offices on Jordan Basin Lane in town. It maintains the main, and the private line from your house to the main is yours.

A slow main drain is a drain cleaning call, but check the cleanout first. The district says standing water at the cleanout points to a blockage between your house and the main, so call the district. A dry cleanout points to your own house plumbing, and that is ours to clear.

Rules and sources behind this page
