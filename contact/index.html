What to tell us when you call

  • ✓Your address. Street and town, so we can confirm we cover it and plan the route.
  • ✓What you see, hear or smell. Spraying water, a hissing pipe, a rotten-egg smell or a gurgling drain each point to a different cause.
  • ✓Where the water shutoff is. If you can find the main valve, we will tell you whether to close it.
  • ✓Whether anyone is in danger. Gas smell, sewage or water near electrical comes first, and we may send you to 911 or the gas utility before anything else.
