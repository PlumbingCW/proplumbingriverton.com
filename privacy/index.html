Home / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Short and plain. This site has no forms. Most information reaches us by phone.

What we collect

When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, service address and a description of the problem. We use this to dispatch a plumber, give you a price and follow up on the job.

This website has no contact forms. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as page requests and technical details of the visiting browser.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the person who answers at the start of the call.

How we use information

We use call details to provide plumbing service, answer questions and improve how we handle calls. We do not use call details for anything unrelated to your service request.

We share information only with plumbers and staff who need it to complete your job, and when the law requires it.

Your choices and contact

To ask what we hold about you or to request removal, email contact@proplumbingriverton.com or call the number in the site footer. Our address is 2600 W 12600 S, Riverton, UT 84065.

Dispatch runs from Riverton and covers South Jordan, Herriman, Bluffdale and Draper.
