Riverton water facts

What Riverton homeowners should know about their pipes

Hard water is normal here

Riverton buys its drinking water wholesale from the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District. The city's 2022 water quality report lists average hardness of about 190 mg/L, roughly 11 grains per gallon, so scale in water heaters and faucets is a real wear factor.

Two water systems, two shutoffs

Culinary water runs inside the home. Secondary irrigation water runs the yard. The city asks residents to shut their secondary valves by March 15 each year, and activation is tentatively set for May 1, so spring is when sprinkler leaks surface.

You own the line from the meter to the house

The city is responsible for the water line up to and including the meter. The pipe between the meter and your home belongs to you, which matters when you find a wet patch in the front yard.

Sewer main versus your lateral

Jordan Basin Improvement District maintains the sewer main. The private line from your house to the main is yours. Standing water at your cleanout points to a blockage between the house and the main.

Before the plumber arrives

What to do while you wait

1

Shut off the water

Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops. For a single fixture, close the valve under it.

2

Stop using drains

If sewage or gray water is backing up, do not flush or run taps. More water only adds to the problem.

3

Mind the electricity

Stay out of standing water near outlets, appliances or a panel. Call the utility or 911 if water has reached live electrical.

4

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and furniture away from the wet zone, and take photos for insurance before cleanup.

How to verify any Utah plumber's license

Utah licenses plumbers through the Division of Professional Licensing. The classes are Apprentice, Journeyman, Residential Journeyman, Master and Residential Master.

The state's license lookup lets you search by name or license number and narrow the profession to plumber. It takes a minute and works for any contractor, not only for us.
