Terms of Use

Plain terms for using this website and calling our line.

Using this website

The content on this site is general information about plumbing. It does not replace an on-site diagnosis. Every home is different, and a plumber needs to see the problem to give you a real answer.

In an emergency involving fire, gas, sewage exposure or injury, call 911 or the gas utility first.

Our service

Service is arranged by phone. We state a price before work starts, and any change in scope is discussed with you before we continue. Availability depends on the time of your call and crew location, and we do not promise arrival times.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information accuracy and links

We work to keep local details accurate, but utility programs, permit rules and codes change. Check with the Riverton building department or your utility before relying on any rule mentioned here.

This site may link to government and utility pages. We do not control those sites.
