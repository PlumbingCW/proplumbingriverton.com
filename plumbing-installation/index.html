Plumbing Installation in Riverton, UT

Plumbing installation in Riverton, UT is the planned work of putting in new water, drain, vent and gas lines and the fixtures that connect to them. It covers remodels, additions, new construction and swapping out old fixtures.

Our licensed plumbers walk the space, mark where each line will run and explain the permit question before anything is cut. You get a clear price before work starts.

Scope

What we install

  • ✓Fixtures:sinks, toilets, tubs, showers, utility sinks, hose bibs and the shutoff valves behind them.
  • ✓Water supply lines:new runs to a bathroom, kitchen island, laundry or accessory space, usually in PEX or copper.
  • ✓Drain and vent lines:slope, venting and cleanouts matter as much as the supply. A bad drain layout causes slow drains for years.
  • ✓Appliance connections:dishwasher, ice maker, washer boxes and garbage disposals tied into supply and drain.
  • ✓Gas lines:new or extended gas piping to ranges, dryers, fire features and heaters, done only by licensed plumbers.

Anything that moves a wall, a sink or a toilet starts as a layout question. We explain what the framing and floor will allow before you buy fixtures, so you do not pick a vanity that fights the drain.

Remodels

Rough-in for a remodel: how the plan comes together

1

Walk the space

We look at the existing supply, drain and vent locations and see what stays and what moves.

2

Pick fixtures first

Fixture spec sheets set the connection points, so choose them before the walls close.

3

Route and mark

We lay out supply and drain runs, plan venting and note where framing or joists need attention.

4

Rough-in and test

We install lines, then pressure test supply and water test drains while everything is still open.

5

Inspection, then close up

If the job needs an inspection, it happens before drywall covers the pipes.

6

Set fixtures and final check

We install fixtures, test every connection and run each one to check for leaks and slow drains.

Island sinks and bathrooms moved to a new wall are the usual trouble spots. They need venting planned in advance, and slab floors limit where new drains can run. We would rather tell you that during the walk-through than after the drywall is up.

Gas safety

Gas line installation and inspection: safety first

If you smell gas, leave the building right away and call Enbridge Gas Utah at 1-800-767-1689 or 911 from a safe place. Do this before you call a plumber. Do not flip switches, use phones inside, or try to find the leak yourself.

Gas piping is not a do-it-yourself project. New lines to a range, dryer or outdoor appliance need correct sizing, secure connections and a leak test before the appliance is lit. We also inspect existing gas connections when you are adding an appliance or you are unsure about an older setup. Natural gas has a sulfur or rotten-egg odorant, and carbon monoxide alarms do not detect it.

Permits

Permits and the plumbing code in Riverton

Riverton's city site says a building permit is required to construct, alter or repair, and for work on electrical, gas, mechanical or plumbing systems regulated by the code. Applications go through the city's building inspections office. It does not say what each small job needs, so ask what yours requires before we start.

Utah Code 15A-2-103 adopts the 2024 International Plumbing Code effective July 1, 2026, with Utah amendments. Those rules shape venting, fixture connections and inspections. You can confirm that any plumber you hire is licensed with the Utah Division of Professional Licensing license lookup at secure.utah.gov/llv/search/index.html, profession PLUMBER.

New builds

New construction and additions

In a new build or addition, plumbing happens in stages: underground rough-in before the slab or foundation, in-wall rough-in at framing, and trim-out when fixtures go in. Each stage has its own inspection, and each depends on the one before it.

The city owns the water line through the meter. Your builder's plumbing connects from the meter to the home, so we coordinate where the service enters the house. Before digging for a new service or sewer lateral, someone must call Blue Stakes at 811.

Sewer for Riverton homes is provided by Jordan Basin Improvement District, which maintains the main, while the private lateral from the home to the main belongs to the owner. We plan drains with that handoff in mind.
