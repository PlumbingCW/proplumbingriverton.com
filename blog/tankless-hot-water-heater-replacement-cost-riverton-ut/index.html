Tankless Hot Water Heater Replacement Cost in Riverton, UT

Three things set the price of a tankless replacement. The first is whether the job swaps a tank for a tankless unit or one tankless unit for another. The second is how much gas and vent work the new unit demands. The third is how hard the installer has to work to get at everything.

We do not print dollar figures here because two houses on the same street can land far apart. What follows is how to read a quote, so you can tell a fair one from a vague one.

Cost drivers, no made-up numbersTank to tankless vs like-for-likeGas, vent and scale

By the Riverton Pro Plumbing team · Published October 29, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

Two different jobs

Replacing a tankless unit is not the same job as converting from a tank

People search for tankless replacement cost and mean one of two things. Some already own a wall-hung unit that has failed, and they want the same kind of heater back. Others own a tank and want to switch. The second job costs more in almost every home, because the plumbing around a tank was never built for a tankless unit.

A like-for-like swap often reuses the gas line, the vent run, the water connections and the isolation valves already on the wall. The plumber mostly removes, mounts and reconnects. A conversion usually means a bigger gas line, a different kind of vent, a condensate drain for some units, and new hot and cold stubs at a different height.

Ask any plumber quoting you which job they believe it is, and why. If the quote does not say whether existing gas and venting will be reused, it is not a real quote yet. The honest answer sometimes arrives only after someone looks at the wall.

Reading the quote

The cost drivers, from biggest swing to smallest

What moves a tankless replacement priceThese factors explain nearly every difference between two quotes for the same house.

Gas line capacity

A tankless burner draws a large burst of gas all at once. If the existing pipe was sized for a tank, a licensed plumber may need to run larger pipe, and that means opening walls or running it along a basement ceiling.

Venting type and route

Some units vent through plastic pipe out a side wall. Others need a different material or a longer run. Every elbow and every foot of vent adds material and labor, and a roof or tight chase adds more.

Unit size and features

A heater sized for one bathroom and a kitchen is a different machine from one sized for a large family with a soaking tub. Recirculation features, condensate handling and app connectivity all change the equipment line on the quote.

Condition of what is already there

Old shutoff valves that will not close, corroded fittings, or a failed pressure relief discharge line all get replaced while the wall is open. These are small items individually and they add up.

Access and disposal

A heater in an unfinished basement is easier to work around than one in a finished closet or garage corner. Hauling away the old unit and any drywall patching are line items worth asking about.

Cost to replace is only part of the picture. Homeowners policies often cover sudden water damage from a failed heater but usually not gradual leaks or wear, so check your own policy. For a real number on your house, call us and a licensed plumber can describe what they would need to see first.

What Riverton homeowners should know

Riverton buys its drinking water wholesale from Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District. Riverton's 2022 water quality report lists average hardness of 190 mg/L, with a range of 174.5 to 202.5 mg/L, which works out to about 11 grains per gallon. That is the figure to use for planning a descaling routine, and it is a 2022 number, so newer Riverton reports may differ.

The practical step is simple. Ask Riverton's water division for its latest annual water quality report, test your own tap with a hardness strip, and plan to flush a tankless unit on a schedule your plumber sets. Hard water does not ruin a tankless heater overnight, but scale is the usual reason performance slips.

Sources: Riverton City 2022 water quality report, Riverton City culinary water page

Scale and service

Why descaling belongs in the cost conversation

A tankless unit heats water as it passes through a narrow heat exchanger. Dissolved minerals in that water can leave scale on the inside of the exchanger. Scale acts like insulation, so the unit works harder, the outlet temperature can wobble, and an error code may appear. Scale builds faster in harder water.

Riverton's own water report lists hard water, and the section at the bottom of this page has the numbers. In practice that means a tankless unit here is not a fit-and-forget appliance. When you compare a quote, check whether it includes isolation valves with service ports. They let a plumber flush the unit with a descaling solution without cutting any pipe.

If your last unit died young, ask the plumber to look at the exchanger and the inlet screen before you buy the same model again. A failure caused by scale is a maintenance story. A failure caused by a cracked exchanger from freezing or a bad vent is a different story, and the replacement plan should change with it.

Before you commit

A fair way to decide between replacing the unit and repairing it

Lean toward repair

  • The unit shows an error code tied to a sensor, igniter or inlet screen
  • Hot water turns lukewarm, but the heat exchanger has never leaked
  • Flushing has never been done and scale is the likely cause
  • The unit is otherwise young and the vent and gas are sound

Lean toward replacement

  • Water is leaking from inside the cabinet or exchanger
  • Repeated failures after repairs, with scale returning fast
  • Vent pipe is cracked, discolored or sagging, which is a safety issue
  • Parts for an older model are hard to source, and the quote for the repair rivals a new install

Our tankless water heater service covers both paths, and a plumber can tell you honestly which side your unit falls on after looking at it. If the answer is a failed tank rather than a tankless unit, the water heater replacement page explains that job and what drives its price.

What to ask

Questions that expose a thin quote

1

Is the existing gas line adequate?

A good answer names the pipe you have and what the new unit needs. A vague answer means the number may change later.

2

What vent material and route will you use?

You should hear where the vent exits and why. Gas appliance venting is a safety item, not a place to improvise.

3

Are service valves included?

Isolation valves with ports make later flushing simple. Without them, descaling is harder and often skipped.

4

What happens to the old heater?

Removal and disposal should be on the quote, along with any wall patching you are expected to handle yourself.

5

Who confirms the permit question?

Permit rules vary, so a plumber should tell you to ask the Riverton building department what your job needs, rather than say yes or no from memory.
