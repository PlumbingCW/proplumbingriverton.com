Epoxy Sewer Pipe Lining Cost in Riverton, UT: What Drives the Price

Epoxy sewer pipe lining coats the inside of a damaged line with a resin liner that hardens into a new pipe within the old one. In Riverton, UT, what you pay depends on how long the run is, how much cleaning it needs first, and whether the pipe is in shape to accept a liner at all.

This guide explains the cost drivers, when lining fits, when digging is the honest answer, and how to compare quotes.

How it works

What epoxy lining does inside a sewer pipe

A crew cleans the pipe, inspects it on camera, then pulls a resin-soaked tube through the damaged section. The tube is inflated against the pipe wall and cured until it hardens. Side connections are reopened afterward with a cutting tool.

The result is a smooth pipe inside the old one. Lining seals small cracks, open joints and root entry points. It does not straighten a sagging pipe, and it does not make a collapsed section whole again.

That last point drives the whole price conversation. Cost depends less on the resin than on what condition the pipe is in before anyone starts.

Price factors

What drives the cost of lining a sewer line

Lining cost driversNo honest plumber can name a number before seeing the inside of your pipe, but these factors shape every quote.

Length of the run

More feet of liner means more material, more curing time and more labor. A short spot liner and a full run to the main are priced very differently.

Pipe diameter and material

Larger pipes need larger liners. Clay, cast iron, cement and plastic each accept the resin differently and may need different prep.

Cleaning before lining

Roots, grease, scale and debris must be removed first, often with hydro jetting or a cable machine. Heavy buildup adds prep time.

Access points

A good cleanout makes the job simple. If there is no usable cleanout, a crew may have to create an access point, which adds work.

Camera and locating

A recorded camera inspection and a locate of the pipe path are part of doing this right and are often priced into the job.

Restoration and reinstatement

Reopening side connections, final camera checks and any cleanup add to the total.

Standard homeowner policies often exclude sewer backup, and slow wear in an old pipe is usually not treated like a sudden burst, so check your policy before you count on coverage. For a real number on your lateral, call us and we will price it after we see the camera footage.

Ask what the liner is expected to do for your pipe, not only what it costs. A liner restores the inner surface and seals leaks, but it adds no strength to soil that is moving and no slope to a pipe that lost it years ago.

Where the pipe is worth saving, lining can save a yard, a driveway and a garden bed. Where it is not, lining wastes money, and an honest installer will say so.

What Riverton homeowners should know

Jordan Basin Improvement District maintains the sewer main, while the private line from your home to the main is your responsibility. That means a lining job on your lateral is normally a homeowner cost, not a district repair. Calling the district at (801) 571-1166 is still the right move if a cleanout shows standing water and you are not sure where the blockage sits.

The district also notes that clay pipes from before the 1980s are prone to roots. If your home is old enough that the lateral could be clay, a camera inspection is the best first step, because root intrusion at joints is exactly what lining can address when the pipe is otherwise intact. Standard homeowner policies often exclude sewer backup, so check yours.

Make a decision

A four-step way to decide between lining and digging

1

Clear the line and camera it

Nothing else makes sense until the inside of the pipe is visible. A sewer camera inspection records cracks, roots, offsets, bellies and the exact distance to each defect.

2

Locate the pipe and the problem

A locator on the surface marks the path and depth. That tells you how much yard, driveway or concrete a dig would cross.

3

Compare the repair paths

Ask for the lining option and the excavation option on the same defect list, so the quotes are comparable. A sewer line repair page can show what spot repairs and full replacement involve.

4

Weigh disruption against condition

If the pipe qualifies, lining usually disturbs less ground. If it does not, a clean dig and replacement is more honest than forcing a liner in.

Compare

Lining versus excavation, side by side

Epoxy lining

  • Smaller entry points, less trench.
  • Works well for cracks, joints and root entry.
  • Requires a pipe that is continuous and mostly round.
  • Cannot fix a bellied or collapsed run.

Dig and replace

  • Opens the ground along the repair length.
  • Fixes belly, offset and collapse directly.
  • Needs Blue Stakes 811 locates before digging.
  • Replaces the section with new pipe.

Before any excavation, call 811 or file a request with Blue Stakes of Utah. Online requests are accepted around the clock, and the notice period counts business days, so build that into your schedule.
