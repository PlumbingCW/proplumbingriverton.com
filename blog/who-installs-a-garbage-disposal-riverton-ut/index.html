Who Installs a Garbage Disposal in Riverton, UT? Plumber, Handyman or Electrician

In Riverton, UT, a licensed plumber is the right call for most garbage disposal installs, because the job connects a new appliance to your sink drain and often to the dishwasher. An electrician enters the picture only when the power side needs work.

The rest of this guide explains the split, what to ask before anyone starts, and how to check a plumber's license for free.

By the Riverton Pro Plumbing team · Published October 29, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

The short answer

What a disposal install actually involves

A disposal looks like a bolt-on appliance, but the install has four separate parts. The mounting flange seals to the sink drain opening, the unit hangs from a mounting assembly under the sink, the discharge tube connects to the drain trap, and the motor needs power.

Add a dishwasher and there is a fifth part. The dishwasher drain hose connects to a nipple on the disposal body, and a knockout plug inside that nipple has to be removed first. Skipping that step is a classic reason a new dishwasher backs up into the sink.

Plumbing putty or a gasket has to seal under the flange without leaking at the sink. Every one of these joints sits above a cabinet floor that absorbs a slow drip for months before anyone notices.

Compare the trades

TaskBest fitWhy it matters

Handymen often do competent small jobs, and some homeowners swap a disposal themselves. The gap shows up in the drain connection and in the permit question, where a general helper may not know the local rules. Whoever you hire, ask who is doing the drain side and who is responsible if it leaks.

Check before you hire

How to verify a plumber's license in Utah

Utah licenses plumbers through the Division of Professional Licensing, usually called DOPL. The plumber classes are Apprentice, Journeyman, Residential Journeyman, Master and Residential Master.

1

Ask for the license number

A legitimate plumber can give you a license number and the name it is held under. If the answer is vague, that tells you something.

2

Open the state lookup

The Utah licensee lookup and verification system is at secure.utah.gov under the llv search page. It is free and does not need an account.

3

Search by name or number

Pick the profession PLUMBER, choose a class if you know it, then search by name or license number.

4

Match the person to the job

Check the status and class. An apprentice works under supervision, so ask who the supervising plumber is if an apprentice shows up.

This takes a couple of minutes and costs nothing. It also shows whether the person at your door holds the license or works for someone who does.

What Riverton homeowners should know

Riverton City says a building permit is required for work on plumbing systems regulated by the code, and it takes applications by email to building@rivertonutah.gov. The city page does not spell out whether a straightforward disposal swap needs one. Ask the Riverton building department before the work starts, and ask your installer who handles it.

The state license lookup is the other local safeguard. Utah's Division of Professional Licensing lets anyone search by name or license number and filter to plumbers, so you can confirm status before a stranger opens your sink cabinet.

Sources: Riverton Building Inspections and Permits page, Utah Division of Professional Licensing license lookup

Planning

What the price depends on

What drives a disposal installThe appliance is only part of the bill, and the sink setup decides most of the labor.

Replacing versus a first install

Swapping an old unit onto an existing mount is quick. A first-time install means cutting the trap, adding fittings and sometimes changing the sink opening.

Motor size and features

Larger motors, sound insulation and continuous-feed or batch-feed designs all change the unit price and the mounting requirements.

Dishwasher connection

Hooking up or reconnecting a dishwasher adds fittings, the knockout removal and a drain loop to prevent backflow.

Existing drain condition

Corroded traps, slow sink drains or odd pipe slopes may need to be corrected so the new unit does not clog right away.

Electrical work

A switch, outlet or cord that is missing or unsuitable adds electrician scope and may add a permit step.

Disposal damage to a cabinet from a leak is rarely covered the way sudden burst-pipe damage can be, so ask your insurer how slow leaks are treated. For a real number on your sink, call us and we will price it before work starts.

If the unit you already have is jammed, humming or leaking, a garbage disposal repair may cost less than a replacement. A technician can tell you which it is after a quick look.

Related work

When a disposal install turns into something bigger

Sometimes the disposal is the smallest part of the job. A remodel that moves the sink, adds an island or changes the drain layout counts as plumbing installation, which has its own planning and permit conversation.

Before any install, shut off power to the unit at the breaker and never reach into a disposal with your hand, even when it looks off. If a unit is jammed, a plumber can turn the motor free from underneath with a hex key and clear what stuck. If the housing itself drips, the usual answer is replacement, since the seals inside are not serviceable.

For a single replacement, the job usually finishes in one visit. We also take calls from South Jordan and Bluffdale, which sit on either side of Riverton, for the same kind of install.

Rules and sources

Rules and sources behind this page

Utah licenses plumbers through the Division of Professional Licensing in classes from Apprentice to Master. Its public lookup is the standard way to confirm who you are hiring.

The plumbing side follows the plumbing code Utah adopts. Utah Code 15A-2-103 adopts the 2024 International Plumbing Code (IPC) as the state plumbing code, effective July 1, 2026, with Utah amendments on top.

Garbage disposal installation questions

Many homeowners do, and a simple swap onto an existing mount is manageable with basic tools. The risk is in the details: a poor flange seal, a skipped dishwasher knockout or a wiring mistake. If you are unsure about any of those, hire a licensed plumber.

Only if the power side needs work. If the sink already has a switch and an outlet or cord connection that suits the unit, a plumber can handle the install. New wiring or a new switch is electrical work.

A replacement onto an existing mount is often finished in one visit. A first-time install with a dishwasher connection and trap changes takes longer. We do not promise timeframes, and we will explain the scope before starting.

The knockout plug inside the disposal's dishwasher nipple may not have been removed, or the drain hose lacks a high loop. Both are simple to fix and both are part of a proper install.

Cooking grease is the big one. It coats pipes downstream and the sewer district asks residents not to pour it down drains even when they have a disposal. Fibrous scraps, bones and large amounts of starchy waste also cause problems.

The city requires a permit for work on plumbing systems regulated by the code, but its page does not address disposal swaps specifically. Ask the Riverton building department, and ask your installer who is responsible for checking.
