Decide

Is it a cleaning problem or a repair problem?

Plenty of sewer backups come from soft clogs that cleaning fixes for good. A repair is different: it addresses a pipe that is cracked, offset, sagging, or rooted at a joint, and it is the reason a cleaned line keeps coming back.

A common case is a localized crack in a plastic sewer pipe with roots growing in, where a spot repair may be enough. A camera pays for itself here, because it shows where the damage is and how far it extends.

  • Backups that return within weeks of a cleaning point to a structural cause.
  • A cable that stops at the same distance every time suggests a break, an offset or a heavy root mass.
  • Soggy or sunken ground along the line can mean a leaking pipe, though it can have other causes.
  • Sewer smell in the yard or basement deserves a camera look, not air freshener.
Urgent

When a broken sewer line is an emergency

Treat it as an emergency when sewage is coming up inside the home, when several fixtures stop draining at once, or when a toilet will not flush and water in the tub rises. Stop all water use right away.

Keep people and pets out of any room with sewage, and don't try to rinse it down a drain. We offer same-day service when available, and we also take after-hours calls on the 24/7 line.

No-dig option

Pipe lining and trenchless repair

Trenchless repair works through the pipe instead of around it. In pipe lining, a resin-saturated liner is pulled into the old pipe and cured in place, forming a new pipe inside the old one. That avoids cutting through a driveway, patio or landscaping.

It is not for every pipe. A line that has collapsed, has a major offset, or has a deep belly usually has to be dug. The camera footage decides which method fits.

Older plumbing

Cast iron pipe repair

Where a home has cast iron drain pipe, the usual problems are rust scale narrowing the bore, cracks, and failed joints. Cast iron can be spot-repaired with new plastic sections, relined, or replaced.

Which one makes sense depends on how much of the run is bad. If the camera shows long stretches of heavy corrosion, replacing the whole run often costs less in the long run than repeated spot fixes.

Local rules

Riverton's sewer setup, roots and insurance

Jordan Basin Improvement District (JBID) maintains the main, and the private line from your house to it is yours to repair. JBID notes that clay pipes from before the 1980s are prone to roots, which matters if your house is that old.

Standard homeowners policies often exclude sewer backup, so ask your insurer before work begins. We can't promise coverage, but we'll explain what the camera found so you can discuss it with them.

We also dispatch to Herriman and Draper, and the same district maintains the sewer mains there.

Septic

Septic systems: only where a property has one

A septic tank is only part of a property that isn't connected to the public sewer. If you're unsure which you have, your property records or JBID at (801) 571-1166 can tell you whether you connect to the district.

If you do have septic, tell us when you call. We'll be honest about which lines and fittings we handle and which jobs need a specialist.
